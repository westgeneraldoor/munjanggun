# Munjanggun Promo Video Design v3

## Style Prompt
A premium dark video for the "Munjanggun Digital Colorbook." High-energy kinetic typography meets luxury restraint. The opening hits hard with impactful number typography, then reveals into immersive full-screen textures and real installation photography. Apple keynote-level polish: deliberate pacing, confident motion, typographic hierarchy that commands attention.

## Colors
- `--bg`: `#0A0A0A` (warm-tinted dark, not pure black)
- `--fg`: `#F5F5F5` (off-white, never pure #FFF for body)
- `--accent`: `#C8A876` (premium sand gold — unified accent throughout)
- `--sub`: `#666666` (muted for secondary text)
- `--bg-card`: `#141414` (elevated surfaces)

## Typography
- Headlines: `Pretendard Variable`, weight 900 (Black)
- Display: `Playfair Display`, weight 600 italic (luxury serif accents)
- Body: `Pretendard Variable`, weight 400-500

## What NOT to Do
- No cyan-on-dark or neon accents — this is luxury, not tech
- No identical entrance patterns within a scene — vary direction and ease
- No pure #000000 background — always tint warm (#0A0A0A)
- No static decoratives — all background elements must have ambient motion
- No linear gradients on large dark areas (H.264 banding risk)
- No exit animations except on the final scene — transitions handle exits
- No `repeat: -1` — calculate finite repeat counts
